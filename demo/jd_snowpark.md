# Senior Data Engineer — Streaming Platform (Fintech)

We are hiring a Senior Data Engineer to own the real-time data platform of a
payments company processing millions of transactions a day.

## What you will do
- Build and own ELT pipelines in **Snowflake** using **Snowpark DataFrames
  (Python)** — dataframe-native transformations, no raw-SQL-only workflows.
- Model data with **dbt** and orchestrate with **Apache Airflow**.
- Build streaming ingestion on **Apache Kafka** with exactly-once semantics
  into Snowflake.
- Provision infrastructure with **Terraform** on AWS.
- Partner with analytics on **Looker** semantic models.

## Requirements
- 7+ years of data engineering experience.
- Deep **Python** and **SQL** expertise.
- Production experience with **Snowpark** is a hard requirement.
- Hands-on **Kafka** streaming experience.
- Experience with **dbt**, **Airflow**, and **Terraform**.
- Nice to have: **Apache Flink**, **Kubernetes**, **CockroachDB**.
